Tutor-Sent vs. Brand-Sent Drift Email
Hypothesis: Emails appearing to come from the student's tutor ("noticed you haven't been in") will outperform brand-sent emails on open rate and re-engagement click because the relationship feels personal, not transactional.
A: From: Nerdy TeamB: From: Sarah (your tutor)

Progress Report Data Density
Hypothesis: Parents who receive a highly specific progress report (session count, score delta, topics covered, next milestone) will have higher renewal rates vs. those who receive a softer narrative summary, because outcome data justifies the spend.
A: Narrative summaryB: Data-first (sessions, scores)C: Data + narrative hybrid

Pause vs. Cancel Landing Page
Hypothesis: Surfacing a "pause for 30 days, free" offer prominently on the cancellation flow will reduce hard cancels by at least 15% by giving cost-sensitive families a lower-stakes option.

Send-Time Optimization: Parents
