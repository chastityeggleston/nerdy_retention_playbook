Optimization Layer

Experiment Backlog

Prioritized A/B and multivariate tests ranked by expected lift vs. implementation effort. Each hypothesis is grounded in observed drop-off patterns.

01
Tutor-Sent vs. Brand-Sent Drift Email
Hypothesis: Emails appearing to come from the student's tutor ("noticed you haven't been in") will outperform brand-sent emails on open rate and re-engagement click because the relationship feels personal, not transactional.
A: From: Nerdy Team B: From: Sarah (your tutor)
High Impact Low effort
02
Progress Report Data Density
Hypothesis: Parents who receive a highly specific progress report (session count, score delta, topics covered, next milestone) will have a higher renewal rate vs. those who receive a softer narrative summary, because outcome data justifies the spend.
A: Narrative summary B: Data-first (sessions, scores) C: Data + narrative hybrid
High Impact Med effort
03
Pause vs. Cancel Landing Page
Hypothesis: Surfacing a "pause for 30 days, free" offer prominently on the cancellation flow will reduce hard cancels by at least 15% by giving cost-sensitive families a lower-stakes option.
A: Cancel flow (current) B: Pause offer prominently featured
High Impact Med effort
04
Winback Incentive Type
Hypothesis: A free month credit will outperform a percentage discount for winback because it removes the month-one barrier (the psychological hurdle for a lapsed subscriber) rather than just reducing cost.
A: 20% off first month B: First month free C: Free first session only
Med Impact Low effort
05
Send-Time Optimization: Parents
Hypothesis: Parental engagement emails sent at 7–8pm local time (after school pickup, dinner, homework) will outperform 9am sends on CTR because parents are in planning mode in the evening, not the morning.
A: 9:00am local B: 7:30pm local
Med Impact Low effort
06
Onboarding Email: Goal-Setting vs. Scheduling CTA
Hypothesis: Asking new subscribers to "set a learning goal" in the D1 email will produce higher engagement than "book your first session" because it builds identity commitment before asking for a calendar action.
A: CTA → Book session B: CTA → Set a goal
Med Impact Low effort
